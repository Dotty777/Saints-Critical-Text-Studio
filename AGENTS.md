# AGENTS.md — Guide for AI Coding Agents

Zero mandatory third-party dependencies; `app.py` (GUI) and `cli.py` talk only to `studio.engine.CriticalTextEngine`.

## Invariants (do not break)

1. **Tests never touch real data.** `tests/test_studio.py` sets `CTS_DATA_DIR` and `CTS_OUTPUT_ROOT` to a temp folder *before* importing `studio`. Keep it that way; any new test module must do the same.
2. **Offline first.** Everything except the cloud brain and Drive sync works with no network. Network code must check `config.offline_locked` and raise `PermissionError`; local LLM endpoints must be loopback-only.
3. **Licence quarantine.** `QUARANTINED_NC` / `DISREGARDED_*` witnesses never enter collation unless a human overrides them.
4. **One edition, one vote.** Digital copies of one printed edition share a family in `catalog.EDITION_FAMILIES`; their weights combine by `max()`. Disagreements *inside* a family are encoding differences, not variants. Swete's edition has three copies: the Amicarelli transcription (`swete_csv`, GPL-3.0, keyed — the only one not from OCR), First1KGreek and Swete Clean (one OCR). Keep the transcription the heaviest copy and first in `ORTHOGRAPHIC_BASE`. When the copies disagree, `_sister_keep` decides which reading stands for the edition, in this order: the reading another edition shares (but one word of the base copy is not dropped for an omission another edition shares — Brenton's joined ἀναμέσον leaves μέσον partnerless); the reading that is another edition's in another spelling; words against a gap when another edition has words there; a sister copy's common form when the base copy's form occurs nowhere else in the corpus and is one or two letters away (a keying slip); else the base copy. Otherwise the edition would vote on both sides of a unit. The lexicon counts the heaviest copy of each family only. The base edition's own word division (ἀνὰ μέσον, ἵνα τί) is never treated as an OCR split in the Greek OT.
5. **Never collate a verse against a different verse.** Witnesses are re-aligned to `catalog.VERSIFICATION_ANCHORS` by content (`collator.align_versification`). Do not go back to matching on verse numbers alone.
6. **Witness text = what the source actually says.** Parsers strip markup but must not substitute editorial readings (e.g. Amiatinus `<rdg>`/`<corr>`/`<supplied>`, CNTR corrector readings).
7. **Nothing disappears silently.** Orthographic decisions go to the disregarded log with a reason; verse-level absences go to the apparatus and the verse is printed in `⟦ ⟧`; AI model failures and rejected proposals are written into the rationale.
8. **AI models may only choose attested wording** (`brains.base.check_model_text_is_attested`) and may only make gatekeeper verdicts stricter (`brains.base.apply_model_gatekeeper_opinion`). Claude (`brains/claude_review.py`) and OpenAI (`brains/openai_review.py`) reviews answer by reading number only and are advisory — keep them that way. OpenAI may abstain; validate exact integer indices and supporting sigla against the current batch. Its model certainty never replaces calibrated engine scores. Missing/rejected answers and failures must remain visible; preserve successful earlier batches if a later one fails.
9. **Internal evidence is explained, not hidden.** The Textual Analysis Brain (`brains/textual_analysis.py`) adds findings to `ApparatusItem.analysis` (canon, note, which reading it favours/disfavours by canonical key, strength). Weight changes go only through `OfflinePhilologicalBrain._internal_evidence` and must each produce a finding.
10. **Whole-verse repairs stay visible.** Word conservation (`_conservation_fix`) and omission credit (`_omission_credit`) in `offline_philological.py` only ever choose among attested readings, write a note into the unit, compare words with the spelling-tolerant `_conservation_key`, and never reopen a unit decided by an OCR repair or override a decision made by witnesses that really lack the word. Restoring words and omission credit run on the Greek NT only; duplicate removal runs everywhere. Versification repairs in `engine.py` (`_split_merged_verses`, `_spill_split`, `_trim_overlong`, `_resolve_break_lacunae`, `_versification_duplicates`) and in `collator.align_versification` (folding) are logged in `versification_notes`; the anchor's own verses are never removed. `_trim_overlong` never trims words the anchor prints within three verses in the same chapter (Exod 40:8 / Brenton 40:10). The Swete transcription's own re-orderings (Rahlfs's order in Dan 3:67–72, Jer 10:5b) are undone in its parser (`_SWETE_CSV_MOVES`, `_SWETE_CSV_SPAN_MOVES`). Known slips of single copies of Swete and Swete's different verse divisions are repaired in `source_repairs.py` only: each entry is exact-match on the copy's own text, idempotent and logged in `versification_notes`; a wrong word becomes `[…]` (no vote, so the other copies decide), lost text `…`, a doubled word is kept once, and a clause moves only to Brenton's verse. The anchor itself gets numbering repairs only, to reach Saints.Bible numbering (Brenton's Proverbs 24:22–62 → 24:22, 30:1–33, 31:1–9). Never supply a word there; add an entry only with the evidence (another copy of Swete, the Larger Cambridge B text) in local verification notes. A spelling that two independent digitisations of the edition share (`catalog.independent_digitisations`: the keyed transcription and the OCR) is the edition's own and is never "corrected" from another edition by the accent-frequency or transcription-error checks. When the base copy skips three or more words that its sister copies have and the other edition prints elsewhere in the same verse (so it only seems to omit them — Josh 18:28), the sister copies' words are kept.
11. **Confidence means something.** `calibrated_unit_confidence` is fitted on the Greek NT; elsewhere it is labelled uncalibrated in the verse's "confidence" finding. Rule-based repairs (OCR, dittography) get 0.9, Hebrew conventions 0.99. Do not go back to averaging raw weights.
12. **One stream map.** `catalog.STREAM_OF_SIGLUM` / `stream_of()` is the only map of witnesses to text streams; the engine and both brains use it.
13. **Print what some witness reads, locally.** In the Greek NT a run of neighbouring variation units whose separately chosen readings no witness has together is decided as a whole (`_attested_run_fix`, canon `alignment: attested combination`). Keep the check; do not let one unit's lemma become another unit's rival word (John 14:7).
14. **OCR repairs need an OCR shape, not just counts.** A genuine rare reading of B occurs once in the corpus, exactly like a garbled word, so `_ocr_word_slip` repairs only changes `_ocr_shaped` accepts (confusable letters, one dropped/added letter, a swap); never preverbs (`_prefix_change`), itacism, movable ν or doubled consonants. Grammar checks (`_subscript_needed`, `_article_agreement_slip`), broken tokens (`_broken_token`), Latin letters (`_latin_letter_damage`) and dittography with loss are the only other repairs, all logged, all limited to a reading of one edition family; `_drop_duplicated_tails` (engine) is Greek OT only. A repetition the other edition also prints (spelt its own way: Gen 11:27 'Θάρα· Θάρα' / 'Θάῤῥα· Θάῤῥα') is never a dittography.
15. **Numerals must match in value and wording.** A numeral mark never licenses collapsing an entire variant unit. Resolve only to an unambiguous attested full-word form (known spelling equivalents allowed); keep different values, cardinal/ordinal or case distinctions, unknown expansions, and changed word order visible. Unresolved numerals have value-specific keys, so η᾽ (eight) is not ἢ (or), and ςʹ (six) is not σʹ (two hundred). Do not read an elision or a stray OCR accent as a numeral. Numbers 26:31/47, Matthew 18:20, and the ambiguous δύο/δευτέρᾳ case are regression guards.
16. **Composed spelling rules need lexical counterexamples.** Matthew 1:18's μνησστευθισησ combines σσ/σ and ι/ει; preserve the first-hand transcription and log the spelling equivalence. Keep λλ/νν tense/word distinctions and Micah 6:7's χειμάρρων/χιμάρων distinct. Spelling-pool logs must name every discarded surface form, including the former leading form when a newly merged form becomes the displayed spelling.
17. **Apparatus witnesses never vote.** The editions in `catalog.APPARATUS_ONLY` (the Majority and ecclesiastical editions added 17 Nov 2026) are re-aligned and recorded by `studio/apparatus_witnesses.py` after the brain has decided the text. Keep them out of `mapped_by_sig`, the lexicon, the base/anchor choice and the Textual Analysis Brain's context. A verse only they print is never printed. Making one of them vote is an editorial decision for the owner: without it, adding such editions shifted 494 NT verses to the Byzantine reading, changed MAM's pointing in 11,500 Hebrew verses, and printed 126 Greek OT verses that B lacks.
18. **Saints Concordance: tagged lemmas, Saints.Bible verse numbers, licence hygiene (`studio/concordance.py`, `studio/concordance_gold.py`, `studio/lexica/`, Tab 5).** Lemmas come only from word-by-word tagged, openly licensed editions aligned to the critical text (OSHB for Hebrew/Aramaic, STEPBible TAGNT for the Greek NT, Open Scriptures LXX lemmas for the Greek OT) or the Lewis & Short lemmatiser (Latin); a word that cannot be resolved stays untagged, never guessed. Root families come only from cited lexica (OSHB/BDB, Strong's CC0, MorphGNT derivations, Lewis & Short etymologies); without such data a lemma is its own family. Every token is filed under its Saints.Bible (Greek/Septuagint) verse via `studio/lexica/versification.py` — Hebrew (MT) and Latin are never paired with Greek by raw verse number. Keep `anchor_verse_id = book_order*10000000 + chapter*10000 + verse*10 + subverse`. Non-commercial or quarantined data (PROIEL, Rahlfs/CCAT, ETCBC, `05_Tier5_*`, `witness-cache/.local-nc-only`) may be used by evaluation scripts outside `studio/` only; STEPBible TBESH/TBESG are read for columns 1-7 only (column 8 'Meaning' needs Online Bible permission). The pack carries attribution and a note of changes (`manifest.json`). Rebuilds keep alignments a person edited in Tab 5 (recorded in `studio_revisions` as `human_editor`); `trg_protect_human_locked_tokens` only blocks changing the review status of a locked token, so code must not rely on it for more. The `editions` gatekeeper triggers guard that table only; what keeps restricted text out of the pack is that the concordance indexes nothing but `verses.critical_text`. Report measured numbers only (the first build showed a hard-coded '99.1 % bloat eliminated'). The seven colour hubs use the site's seven-colour key (`concordance_gold.HUB_KEY`); a hub unit gets a specific sense only when one of its words decides it (`CURATED_HUB_MEMBERS` offset), otherwise the hub's 'sense not yet chosen' row (offset 64) until a person sets it. Pack licences: Hebrew CC BY 4.0; Greek and Latin CC BY-SA 4.0 (MorphGNT, Lewis & Short).

## Extending

- **New brain:** subclass `CriticalBrainProvider`, register in `studio/brains/__init__.py`, add to `BRAIN_OPTIONS` in `app.py`. Reuse the two guards above.
- **New witness format:** add a book-level parser returning `{chapter: {verse: text}}` to `studio/parsers.py` and dispatch it in `_load_book_cached`. Use `VERSE_ABSENT` ("") for "extant but lacks the verse", `LACUNA_MARK` ("…") for lost text inside a verse, and `⟦…⟧` for verses an edition brackets as later additions.
- **Changing built-in witness descriptions:** edit `seed_default_witnesses()` in `studio/db.py` and bump `SEED_VERSION` so existing databases refresh (rows with `human_override = 1` are left alone).
- **Corpus & edition locations:** all witnesses and critical-text outputs live self-contained inside `Saints-Critical-Text-Studio/Saints-<Language>-Critical-Text` (including `Saints-Latin-Critical-Text/saints-latin-column`). Build witness paths from `catalog.GREEK/HEBREW/LATIN_RESEARCH_ROOT` and `LATIN_TRANSLATION_ROOT`; `parsers.resolve_corpus_path` re-finds paths stored under older layouts so databases keep working.
- **Measuring a change to the Greek OT:** dump every verse before and after in a sandbox (`CTS_DATA_DIR`/`CTS_OUTPUT_ROOT` in a temp folder, `eng.config.provider_id = "offline_philological"`, `collate_and_adjudicate("greek", slug, ch)` for every chapter), diff the texts and have the changed verses judged against the witnesses. Spot checks have repeatedly missed regressions.
